ABOUT

Hi, I'm Keshav. I currently work in Operational Technology (OT) cybersecurity, helping secure industrial environments while constantly exploring different areas of security outside of work. My goal is to become a security researcher, and this blog is where I document that journey. Whenever I come across something interesting, a detection technique, a Windows internals concept, a piece of malware, or even a mistake that taught me something, I try to write about it here. Writing helps me understand things better, and if these posts end up helping someone else along the way, that's even better. I'm still learning, so if you spot something I could have done differently or have a better approach, I'd love to hear it. Cybersecurity grows because people share what they learn, and I hope this little corner of the internet can contribute to that. Reach me out on my email - kdkapil02@gmail.com
