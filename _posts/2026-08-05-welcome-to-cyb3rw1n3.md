## Why this site exists

Cybersecurity is a field where continuous learning is essential. This site exists to document my research, experiments, and observations as I explore detection engineering, threat intelligence, malware analysis, incident response, and defensive security. Every article reflects something I've investigated, built, or learned through hands-on experimentation.

## What to expect here?

Cybersecurity is a field where there's always something new to learn, and that's one of the reasons I enjoy it so much. I'm currently working in OT cybersecurity, and I aspire to become a security researcher. This blog is my way of documenting that journey. Every post is based on something I've been exploring, building, or trying to understand—from detection engineering and threat hunting to Windows internals and malware analysis. My hope is that these write-ups not only reinforce my own learning but also help others who are curious about the same topics. I also believe some of the best learning comes from sharing ideas, so I look forward to the discussions, feedback, and perspectives that the cybersecurity community has to offer.
