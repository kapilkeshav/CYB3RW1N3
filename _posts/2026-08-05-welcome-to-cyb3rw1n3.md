## Why this site exists

This is an example article. Your actual research posts will be Markdown files stored inside the `_posts` folder.

## Publishing is intentionally simple

Create a file using this naming format:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md
```

Paste the template, replace its metadata, write your research, and push it to GitHub.

The landing-page card is generated automatically from the post's title, description, category, and date.
